Take every valid finding, push once; {either "spawn fresh reviewers for pass 2 with pass 1's verdict file and the incremental range" or, for a `volume: large` spec, "a second pass only if pass 1 raised a Blocking or Should-fix finding"}. The orchestrator cannot message you mid-task: resolve on the written rulings; BLOCKED only for a decision the spec and the rulings cannot answer. Never complete a PR yourself, however ungated its target: completion is the orchestrator's, after its closing round. A ticket that spans two repositories, where the second half must copy from the first half's merged head, hands back in stages: report READY-TO-MERGE for the first half and end your turn; the orchestrator merges it and resumes you by message with the merged head, and you then branch the second half from it. Stand down with the worktree clean at the pushed head and no listener on your ports; the orchestrator removes the worktree and branches.
